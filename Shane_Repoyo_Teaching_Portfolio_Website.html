02 / Teaching approach

A classroom built for participation.

My approach combines structure with play and practice. These are the principles I aim to bring to a learning environment.

01 — CONNECT

Build trust first

Use warm greetings, predictable routines, and clear expectations so children understand what to do and feel comfortable joining in.

02 — ENGAGE

Make language active

Use songs, visuals, movement, games, and short practice cycles to give learners several ways to understand and use new English.

03 — REFLECT

Notice every step

Observe participation, listen to student responses, and adjust the level of support so learners can practise successfully and grow.

04 / Sample resources

Small steps. Meaningful practice.

These original examples demonstrate how I might structure an English lesson and a simple learner activity. They are portfolio samples, not claims that these exact materials were used in a past class.

Sample lesson plan · Ages 6–8

Hear it, build it, read it

Focus: Beginning sounds and simple CVC words.

Learning goal: Learners will identify a beginning sound and blend three sounds to read a short word.

  • Warm-up (5 min): Name familiar picture cards and listen for first sounds.
  • Model (5 min): Say each sound slowly, then blend it into a word.
  • Guided practice (10 min): Match picture cards to words with a partner.
  • Check-in (5 min): Learners read one word or point to the matching picture.

Support: Picture cues and teacher modelling. Extension: Say a sentence using a target word.

Sample learner worksheet

Read, match, and try

Say the word aloud. Match it to a picture, then practise writing the missing letter.

1. Read these words
catsunmap
2. Add the missing sound

c a   s n   m a

3. Draw one word you read

Quick assessment: Can the learner say the word, identify its sounds, and attempt the missing letter?
